# Strategy & Positioning

## Positioning Statement

**GET** is the strategic automation partner for scaling B2B technology companies seeking to transform their digital operations into competitive advantages. We bridge the gap between ambitious growth goals and operational reality by building bespoke digital products, intelligent automation systems, and data-driven experiences that compound in value over time.

Unlike generalist agencies or boutique design studios, GET combines deep technical expertise with strategic business acumen to deliver solutions that don't just look impressive—they measurably improve efficiency, reduce costs, and unlock new revenue streams.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Primary Persona: Scaling B2B Tech Leader

**Profile:** VP of Operations, Head of Digital, or CTO at a Series B+ technology company (50-500 employees) experiencing growing pains from manual processes and fragmented systems.

**Industries:** SaaS, Fintech, HealthTech, E-commerce platforms, B2B marketplaces

**Company Characteristics:**
- Revenue: $10M-$100M ARR
- Growing 30-100% YoY
- Engineering/product team stretched thin
- Operations becoming bottleneck to growth
- Multiple disconnected tools (CRM, ERP, custom apps)
- Leadership recognizes automation as strategic priority

### Top 3 Pain Points & Solutions

#### Pain Point #1: Operational Bottlenecks Stalling Growth
**The Problem:** Manual processes, hand-offs between teams, and data silos are creating operational debt. Customer onboarding takes weeks, reporting is spreadsheet-heavy, and teams spend 40%+ of time on repetitive tasks instead of strategic work.

**GET's Solution:** We audit your operational stack, identify automation opportunities, and build integrated workflows that eliminate friction. From customer onboarding to reporting to inventory management—we transform operations into a competitive advantage.

**Proof Points:**
- Reduced customer onboarding time from 14 days to 3 days for Series B SaaS client
- Automated 85% of manual reporting for Fintech platform, saving 120+ hours/month
- Built unified data pipeline connecting 7 disconnected systems

#### Pain Point #2: Technical Debt & Fragmented Systems
**The Problem:** Years of rapid growth have created a patchwork of custom apps, third-party tools, and legacy systems that don't communicate. Technical debt is slowing new feature development, data integrity is questionable, and scaling requires heroic efforts from the engineering team.

**GET's Solution:** We don't just build new solutions—we strategically consolidate and integrate. We modernize legacy systems, build elegant bridges between tools, and create architectures that scale. Every project considers technical debt reduction alongside feature delivery.

**Proof Points:**
- Consolidated 4 customer data platforms into single source of truth
- Refactored 5-year-old legacy codebase, reducing deployment time from 2 hours to 8 minutes
- Built API integration layer enabling 3x faster feature development

#### Pain Point #3: Digital Experiences Not Converting
**The Problem:** Marketing spends significant budget driving traffic, but digital experiences don't convert at optimal rates. Product interfaces are functional but not frictionless. Customer portals exist but don't drive engagement. Growth is left on the table.

**GET's Solution:** We design and build digital products and experiences optimized for conversion and retention. We use data to identify drop-off points, run experimentation programs, and continuously optimize. Our products don't just work—they drive business outcomes.

**Proof Points:**
- Redesigned customer portal resulting in 2.3x increase in feature adoption
- Built self-service onboarding flow reducing support tickets by 67%
- A/B testing program increased trial-to-paid conversion by 41%

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Core Attributes
- **Confident but not arrogant:** We know our craft and communicate with certainty, but never dismiss others
- **Strategic first, technical second:** We start with business value, not features
- **Concise and direct:** We respect our audience's time—no fluff
- **Human and approachable:** We're experts, but we're also real people who enjoy collaboration
- **Results-oriented:** Every message ties back to business outcomes

### Voice Characteristics

**Tone Spectrum:**
- Professional (75%) – Appropriate for B2B contexts, serious but not stiff
- Energetic (60%) – Convey momentum and excitement about solving problems
- Empathetic (50%) – We understand the pain and frustration our clients feel
- Authoritative (70%) – We're experts and our advice should be trusted

**Do:**
- Use active voice: "We build systems that scale" not "Systems are built"
- Be specific: "Reduced onboarding by 40%" not "Improved onboarding"
- Ask questions: "What's your biggest operational bottleneck?"
- Use second person: "Your team deserves better tools"
- Include concrete examples: "Like the client who saved 200 hours/month..."

**Don't:**
- Use jargon without explanation
- Make unsubstantiated claims
- Use buzzwords (synergy, paradigm shift, game-changer)
- Be overly casual or slangy
- Talk about ourselves more than client outcomes

### Writing Guidelines

**Headlines:**
- 6-12 words maximum
- Benefit-focused, not feature-focused
- Use strong verbs: Transform, Eliminate, Accelerate, Unlock
- Avoid "We" phrasing: "Better Operations" not "We Build Better Operations"

**Subheads:**
- 12-20 words maximum
- Elaborate on headline with specific benefit or proof
- Include numbers when possible: "2x faster customer onboarding"

**Body Copy:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- One idea per paragraph
- Use bullet points for lists
- Bold key phrases for scannability
- Link to deeper content for details

**Calls-to-Action:**
- Action-oriented verbs: Schedule, Explore, Calculate, See
- Include value proposition: "Schedule a free automation audit"
- Create urgency when appropriate: "Limited spots for Q1 strategy sessions"
- Secondary CTAs for lower commitment: "Download our automation checklist"
